##1. What is Data Visualization?
Data visualization is the process of representing data in a visual form, such as charts, graphs, maps, and dashboards, to make information easier to understand.
*Example: In your Sales Data Analysis project, you can use a bar chart to compare product sales and a line chart to show sales trends over time.
##Types of Data Visualization
##1. Bar Chart
Compares values across different categories.
Example: Compare sales of different products.
##2. Line Chart
Shows changes and trends over time.
Example: Monthly sales from January to December.
##3. Pie Chart
Displays proportions of a whole.
Example: Percentage of total sales by category.
##4. Histogram
Shows the distribution of numerical data.
Example: Distribution of product prices.
##5. Heatmap
Uses colors to represent values or relationships.
Example: Correlation between sales, profit, and quantity.
##6. Box Plot
Summarizes the distribution of numerical values and helps identify potential outliers.
Example: Find unusual values in sales data.

- Makes complex data easier to understand.
- Helps identify trends and patterns.
- Supports better decision-making.
